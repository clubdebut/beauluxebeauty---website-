Laser Treatments

Advanced laser solutions for unwanted pigment, tattoo removal and long-term hair reduction, using results-driven technology and customized treatment plans. Available at our Oregon location.

Oregon location

Restorative or corrective — always planned.

Whether restorative or corrective, our laser services are designed with precision, safety and efficiency in mind. The number of sessions you need depends on the treatment area, your skin and your goals — which is exactly what the consultation is for.

Laser work also pairs naturally with the rest of the BioBeauty ecosystem, and is often the first step clients take before moving into skin regeneration or aesthetic artistry.

What we offer

Two core services

Hair reduction

Laser Hair Removal

Long-term hair reduction for single areas, paired areas or full body, delivered as a planned course of sessions spaced several weeks apart. Fewer ingrowns, less maintenance and noticeably more comfortable skin.

Package of sessions

Correction

Tattoo Removal

Fading and removal of unwanted tattoos, including correction of previous permanent makeup. Treatment is staged across multiple sessions to let the body clear pigment between visits.

Staged sessions

Session counts and suitability are confirmed at consultation, where we assess skin type, the treatment area and your goals.
