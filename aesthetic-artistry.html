Injectable Artistry

Injectable Artistry is a customized approach to facial balancing using advanced techniques with dermal fillers and neurotoxins. The focus is on enhancing your natural features while maintaining harmony and softness.
Our philosophy is rooted in refined, undetectable results. Each treatment is strategically placed to enhance — not alter — your natural appearance.
Every face is different, which is why we begin with a consultation to assess structure, symmetry and your aesthetic goals before creating a treatment plan.
Most treatments require minimal downtime, with some mild swelling or bruising possible depending on the areas treated.
Questions

PMU Artistry

Permanent Makeup (PMU) is a cosmetic tattooing technique used to enhance brows, lips and eyes with long-lasting, natural-looking definition.
Our approach is soft, refined and tailored to your features — designed to enhance rather than overpower your natural beauty.
Results can last several years, with recommended touch-ups to maintain colour and shape over time.
There is minimal downtime, though slight healing and colour softening will occur in the days following treatment.
